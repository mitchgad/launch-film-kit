# BRAND: brand kit

Everything the build session knows about the company comes from this folder, and every claim in the film has to
trace to it. Record where each item came from and the date you captured it.

## What the company is

- **Tagline and positioning line**, word for word from the site.
- **What the product does**, in the company's own words: meta description, feature names, the lines under each
  feature.
- **Who it's for**, and the problem it solves, quoted from the site.
- **Proof the company states publicly**, such as customers, numbers or awards, each quoted with its page. Leave out
  anything you can't quote.
- **Names to avoid:** real organisations or people in demo data or screenshots that mustn't appear as users.

## Files

- `site/`: a screenshot of every page as a visitor sees it, plus its text in `site/<page>.md`.
- `assets/`: product screenshots and any footage or images the company supplies, each with its licence status.
  Screenshots show what the product does; they're reference for the film's mockup, not layouts to copy.
- `logo/`: the logo files, and how the logo may be used.
- `fonts/`: the brand's fonts, with their licences.
